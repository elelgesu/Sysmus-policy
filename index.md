# Need a hand?

Questions, problems or feedback: email [CONTACT EMAIL]. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

- **No account.** Sysmus keeps your data on your iPhone. To remove all of it, open You > Delete all my data.
- **Subscriptions** are managed by Apple: on your iPhone, Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Refunds: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Timetable not read correctly?** In the review screen, open the class and fix it, or use Re-read with a hint. You can always enter classes by hand.
