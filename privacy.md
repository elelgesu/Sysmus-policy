# Sysmus Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Sysmus is an iPhone app that plans your week around your class schedule. It
is made by Sysmus ("we"). This policy explains what the app
does with your information. In short: your data stays on your device or iPad,
except what Sysmus sends to an AI service from Google to read a timetable,
plan your week and write your weekly review. Our server passes those
requests on without keeping them.

## What we collect

Almost nothing. Sysmus has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking. AI requests pass through a small server of ours on their way
to Google; it does not store or log what you send. To keep the service fair
it counts requests per install, using a random code made by the app that is
not linked to you or your Apple ID. On iPhones that support it, the app
also uses Apple's App Attest to prove a request comes from the genuine app:
our server keeps that app's public attestation key and a request counter,
nothing that identifies you. We do not sell or share your information.

## What stays on your device

Sysmus keeps the following only on your device or iPad, in the app's own storage:

- Your class timetable, your terms (dates, finals and breaks), deadlines
  and other events you add.
- Your answers to the setup interview: sleep and wake times, meals,
  commute, activities, energy through the day, goals, distractions and
  places you work.
- Your weekly plans, edits, daily check-ins (including sleep, energy and
  focus ratings and any notes you write) and weekly reviews.
- Your settings.
- Photos or PDFs of your timetable, only if you choose to keep them for
  reference.
- Backup files Sysmus writes to Files > On My iPhone > Sysmus.

Your device's own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include this data,
depending on its settings. If you copy a Sysmus backup file
elsewhere, that copy is yours to manage.

## What leaves your device

Sysmus plans with AI from Google. When you use these features, Sysmus
sends, over an encrypted connection through our server to Google:

| Feature | What is sent |
|---|---|
| Reading a timetable | The photos, screenshots or PDF pages you choose, once. They are not sent again. |
| Profile brief | Your class times and free time, sleep, energy, activities, goals, deadlines and settings. |
| Generating a plan | Your free time, settings, activities and goals. |
| Ask Sysmus and re-planning | Your sentence and the next seven days of your plan, including class titles. |
| Sunday review | The week's numbers computed on your device, the patterns found, your settings, a short profile and next week's plan. Your check-in notes only if you turn on "Use my notes in the Sunday review". |

Sysmus never sends your name, email address, contacts, location, device
identifiers or your Calendar events. Google processes this content under
its [API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and [Privacy
Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Do not include anything in a
note or a timetable photo that you would not want Google to see. Without a
connection, Sysmus still plans your week and writes reviews on your device.

## Permissions

- **Calendars** (only if you import from Calendar): Sysmus reads the events
  of the calendars you pick. It never changes or adds events, and calendar
  events never leave your device.
- **Camera** (only if you scan a paper timetable).
- **Photos and Files**: only the items you pick.
- **Notifications** (only if you allow them): reminders are scheduled on
  your device. They, the widget and the Live Activity can show block titles
  on your Lock Screen.

Calendar files (.ics) you import are read on your device and are not sent
anywhere.

## Children

Sysmus is made for college students and is not directed to children under
13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your choices

- Keep your check-in notes on your device: leave "Use my notes in the
  Sunday review" off in You > Notifications.
- Delete everything Sysmus stores on your device in You > Delete all my
  data. It removes your data, backups in the Sysmus folder, cached answers
  and timetable images, and cannot be undone.
- Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to
access, correct or delete on your behalf. For what Google holds, see
Google's privacy controls.

## Purchases

If you buy a subscription or pass, Apple processes the payment. We do not
receive your payment details. To know whether your trial, subscription or
pass is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat
receives your purchase history from Apple and a random ID the app makes
for it; this ID is not your name, email, Apple ID or a device identifier.
RevenueCat handles it under its [Privacy
Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Without a purchase, nothing
about you is sent to RevenueCat beyond that random ID and the prices it
shows you.

## California and other US state privacy rights

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not collect it.
California residents and residents of other states with privacy laws have
rights to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of
its sale or sharing; since we hold none, these requests have nothing to act
on, but you may still contact us.

## Security

Sysmus's data files use iOS file protection. Connections to our server
and to Google use HTTPS, and the key to Google's service stays on our
server, never in the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for
significant changes, tell you in the app.
